Editing

Having a good editor, sound designer, and colorist is essential to making your film look good. A lot can change in the edit, and there are still a lot of creative decisions to be made at this stage. Two completly different movies can be made with the same footage but two different editors.

Marketing

Marketing your movie is an important step. Here are some ways your could go about it.

  • hang flyers around buildings on your campus
  • market on Instagram
  • mention it in a film class/club

Screening

Last but not least, it's time to screen your hard work! There are a lot of places on campus you could screen your work, such as lecture halls, classrooms, or even an oncampus movie theater! Make your movie premier fun! Dress up! Make popcorn! Invite your friends! Celebrate all your hard work, and don't forget to get a cast and crew photo.
