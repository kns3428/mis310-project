The Basics of Pre-Production

There are 4 main aspects of pre-production and we will go through each in detail:

  1. Finding a crew
  2. Screenwriting
  3. Location scouting
  4. Sets, props and costumes
  5. Equipment

Finding a Crew

Finding a crew is easier than you think. For a small student film, yoru crew could just be a group of friends who are excited about your project.

There are several crew positions that are essential, as they lead departments:

  1. Director - there vision is what is going to get this film made!
  2. Producer - handles logistics such as locations, budgeting, findng equipment, and feeding everyone on set
  3. Director of Photography (DP) - Head of camera department, works with director to set up shots
  4. 1st Assistant Director (1st AD)- Timekeeper, makes sure everyone knows what they are doing to get this thing done
  5. Key Grip - Leader of lighting department
  6. Art Director - Leader of art department, sets, costumes, and props

In each department there are many roles, but the size of your film will determine how many of these you have

  1. Directing - 2nd AD
  2. Producing - location scouts, Key Personal Assistant, PA team
  3. Director of Photography (DP) - Camera Oporator, 1st Assistant Camera, 2nd AC
  4. Lighting - Best boy, grips
  5. Art - Costume Director, Set Designer, Make-up and Hair, Dressers, Graphic Designers, Choreographer
  6. Editing - Lead Editor, Assistant Editor, Composer, Colorist
