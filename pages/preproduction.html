Screenwriting

In my opinon, this is the fun part. You can kinda write whatever you want, and tell the story you really want to tell. Keep in mind what is feesable for your budget as you write, such as number of actors, locations, or special effects you include.

Proper screenwriting format is also important. There are a free ones; such as YouMeScript.

The most important thing to remember with screenwriting is that you can only write what is going to be on screen. You can't write about what a charater is thinking or feeling. It takes some creativity to figure out how to show character's inner thoughts on screen.
