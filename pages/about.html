Mission Statement

This website exists to give fellow student filmmakers a resource on the basics of student filmmaking. Think of it like filmmaking for dummies. You can use this site to make your own student films, start a filmmaking club at your school, or just to review for those all important film exams. I hope this resource helps at least one student to feel less lost in this crazy world we call student filmmaking.
